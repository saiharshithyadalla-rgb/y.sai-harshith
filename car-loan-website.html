Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How is my car loan EMI calculated?

EMI depends on the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly instalments. Use the calculator above for an estimate.

Can I apply for a used car loan?

Many lenders offer used-car financing, but eligibility, vehicle-age limits, rates and tenure can differ.

Does submitting this form approve my loan?

No. This is a front-end demonstration only. A real lender must verify your application and issue a formal approval or sanction.

What affects car loan eligibility?

Lenders may consider income, existing obligations, credit history, employment or business stability, down payment and vehicle details.
